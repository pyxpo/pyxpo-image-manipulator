# Code of conduct

This project is a place where people of every background and experience level can ask questions and build together.

- Be kind and patient, and assume good intent.
- Give feedback on the code, not the person.
- No harassment, insults, or personal or sexual attention, in any project space or public space where you speak for
  the project.

Report a problem privately to the maintainers through the contact on [github.com/pyxpo](https://github.com/pyxpo).
Maintainers may remove comments, close issues, or block people who break these rules.
